Fee schedule

Fixed values, shared across all scenarios and teams. Edit once here.

Fee amounts shipped with this file are placeholders. C882 is used as the palliative subsequent visit code since most of the group bills as GPs; specialists billing C982 should be modelled separately if the mix is material. E082 stands in for the most responsible physician premium and applies to a set percentage of inpatient subsequent visits. SVP is a single blended evening/weekend premium and is no longer applied to home visits by default. K023 units are full half-hour units, claimable once half the unit is reached. G511, K071 and K072 are not eligible for payment in a week where G512 is billed. CPOC and BBOC are entered as flat annual amounts per physician; confirm the actual per-MD distribution with your call group, since it depends on group size and roster. "Current" approximates today's unstructured model within the same service-line framework and will not match your remittance data — calibrate before relying on the comparison. Patient volumes, patients per FTE, and headcounts are planning estimates entered by you. This model projects gross billings, with no allowance for overhead, taxes, or corporate structure.
