Fee schedule

Fixed values, shared across all scenarios and teams. Edit once here.

Fee amounts shipped with this file are placeholders. C882 is the palliative subsequent visit code for GPs; specialists billing C982 should be modelled separately. A new hospital consult is modelled as A945 (50-minute consultation); subsequent visits as K023 for the greater part of each 20-minute period after, per your specification, with an MRP premium (E082) applied to the MRP subset. K121 and K700 are case conference / rounds codes billed per unit; enter units per week to reflect your team's rounding practice. Community new consults are A945 plus optional K023 and the home visit premiums B966 and B998; community follow-ups are K023-based with the home premiums. Bullet or educational rounds are not case conferences under the Schedule — K121 and K700 require a most-responsible physician and at least two other participants discussing an individual patient, with record and time-keeping requirements. G511, K071 and K072 are not eligible in a week where G512 is billed. CPOC and BBOC are flat annual amounts per physician; confirm the per-MD distribution with your call group. "Current" approximates today's unstructured model and will not match your remittance data — calibrate before relying on the comparison. This model projects gross billings only, with no allowance for overhead, taxes, or corporate structure.
