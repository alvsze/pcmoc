Fee schedule

Fixed values, shared across all scenarios and teams. Edit once here. K301 pays at 85% of the base code — the model applies that automatically to virtual clinic follow-ups.

Rates from MOH Schedule of Benefits, effective April 1, 2026. E083 is 30% of C882 and updates automatically when C882 changes.

All figures are estimates. Rates are from the MOH Schedule of Benefits effective April 1, 2026. E083 is a 30% add-on to C882 (not a flat fee) — at current rates C882 + E083 = $52.07 versus K023 at $85.25, so K023 is the higher-value code for visits of 20 minutes or more. Visit types are entered as counts per week, not minutes. New consults: A945 + K023. Inpatient follow-ups: C882 (under 20 min) or K023 (20 min or more). MRP/PCU patients: C882 + E083 or K023 (toggle). Clinic in-person follow-up: K023. Clinic virtual follow-up: K023 + K301 at 85% of K023. Clinic phone follow-up (under 10 min): A102, a limited phone virtual care code paying a flat rate; A102 has the phone identifier built in so K300/K301 are not added separately — note that A102 is a limited virtual care code intended for patients not seen in person within the preceding 24 months, so for most established palliative patients the comprehensive virtual care route (K023 + K301) may be more appropriate and better compensated. K301 is a modality indicator (telephone), and payment rules require an existing physician-patient relationship and the patient seen in person within the preceding 24 months. Home visits: A945 + K023 (new) or K023 (follow-up), plus B966 and B998. First hospice visit: A945 + K023 + B966 + B998. Hospice follow-up: K023. Outpatient rounds: K700. Note that G512 and its associated management fees (G500, G512, K090, K091 etc.) should be used for phone management rather than virtual care codes — telephone management of patients is already included in G512. K121 and K700 are case conference codes requiring MRP participation and at least two other participants with time-keeping; bullet rounds or educational rounds do not qualify. The FTE slider scales all income linearly, which is an approximation — on-call income may not scale proportionally depending on your group's call distribution. "Current" approximates the unstructured model and will not match remittance data. Gross billings only; no overhead, tax or corporate structure allowance.
